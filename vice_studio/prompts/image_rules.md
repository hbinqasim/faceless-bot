# Universal Image Generation Rules

These rules apply to all Vice Studio image generation agents. Follow these guidelines for consistent, professional, safe, and compelling visual content.

---

## Cinematic Framing

- **Vertical 9:16 aspect ratio** for all Shorts/Reels content
- **Rule of thirds composition** - place focal points at intersections, avoid centered subjects
- **Leading lines** - use roads, horizons, architectural elements to guide viewer's eye
- **Depth composition** - clear foreground, subject, and background separation
- **Frame within frame** - use windows, doorways, architectural elements to create layered depth
- **Avoid flat framing** - always establish spatial depth and dimension

---

## Photorealism

- **Ultra-detailed textures** - every surface shows realistic material quality (metal wear, fabric weave, concrete texture)
- **Natural lighting simulation** - light behaves physically accurate (no impossible shadows or light sources)
- **Physically accurate shadows** - consistent light direction, proper shadow falloff and umbra/penumbra
- **Realistic reflections** - wet surfaces, glass, metal reflect light believably
- **Depth of field** - appropriate focus pull based on cinematic depth and subject importance
- **No artificial smoothing** - maintain texture detail and surface imperfections

---

## Lighting Design

- **Dramatic 3-point lighting** - key light (main), fill light (shadow detail), back light (separation/rim)
- **Neon reflections** - use neon signs and artificial light for atmospheric color and mood
- **High-contrast shadows** - drama and visual interest through light/dark ratio (not flat or even)
- **Motivated lighting** - all light sources must originate from visible or logical scene elements
- **Color temperature variation** - cool and warm lights create visual depth (blue shadows with warm highlights, etc.)
- **Practical lights** - use street lamps, neon signs, headlights, windows as real light sources in scene

---

## Composition & Balance

- **Clear center of interest** - viewer immediately knows where to look
- **Visual balance and weight** - subjects positioned for dynamic yet stable composition
- **Framing that guides eye** - lines, contrast, and focus direct viewer through scene
- **Avoid clutter and confusion** - every element serves purpose, nothing gratuitous
- **Diagonal compositions** - more dynamic than horizontal/vertical (when appropriate)
- **Negative space** - use empty space intentionally to emphasize subject and breathing room

---

## Atmosphere & Mood

- **Establish mood through color** - warm/cool tones convey emotion (orange = energy, blue = calm)
- **Lighting creates atmosphere** - harsh light = intensity, soft light = intimacy, neon = danger/excitement
- **Weather conveys emotion** - rain = sadness/mystery, clear = clarity/hope, haze = uncertainty
- **Environmental storytelling** - scene details suggest narrative (debris = conflict, order = control)
- **Consistent time of day** - maintain logical progression (no impossible sun angles)
- **Authentic environment details** - add believable objects, signage, vegetation for realism

---

## Camera Language

- **Shot type motivates narrative** - wide = establishing context, close = emotional connection, medium = relationship
- **Camera movement supports pacing** - slow push = tension, whip pan = action, static = control
- **Framing emphasizes importance** - larger frame = more important, edge placement = peripheral/less important
- **Angle psychology** - high angle = vulnerability/weakness, low angle = power/dominance, level = equality
- **Focal length choice** - 35mm = neutral, 50mm = intimate, 85mm+ = compressed/romantic, 24mm = wide/establishing
- **Perspective authority** - first-person = immersion, third-person = observer, elevated = omniscient

---

## Vertical 9:16 Specifics (YouTube Shorts, TikTok)

- **Full use of vertical space** - composition fills entire 9:16 frame, not pillarboxed
- **Subject positioned vertically** - accommodate vertical framing, don't crop awkwardly
- **Text placement** - if absolutely necessary, top 1/4 or bottom 1/4, never center
- **Safe title area** - avoid critical action in top/bottom 10% (on-device UI may obscure)
- **Readable on phone** - detail and text must be legible on 5-6 inch mobile screen
- **Avoid horizontal emphasis** - don't create compositions that emphasize horizontal flow (wastes vertical space)

---

## Safety & Compliance Rules

❌ **NEVER include:**

- **Text overlays** - no titles, captions, watermarks, logos, branding within the image itself
- **Watermarks or logos** - no copyrighted branding, no watermarks
- **Official UI elements** - no HUD, no official game interface, no official menus
- **Real celebrity faces** - no recognizable real people without explicit permission
- **Exact official screenshots** - no direct copies of published game imagery
- **Trademarked symbols or logos** - no Nike swoosh, no McDonald's arches, no brand logos
- **Branded product placement** - no prominent product logos or sponsorship visuals
- **Official GTA text or imagery** - no "GTA" logo, no official Rockstar branding
- **Copyrighted music visual references** - no album art, concert footage, artist likenesses

✅ **ALWAYS:**

- Describe **original, inspired** content
- Reference **aesthetic and style**, not exact replicas
- Use **generic architectural and environmental elements**
- Create **inspired Miami crime drama** visuals, not official footage
- Maintain **plausible deniability** - could be any crime drama, not specifically official content

---

## Character Consistency

- **Consistent appearance across scenes** - same character always has same face, build, hair
- **Recognize facial features** - key identifying characteristics (scars, marks, distinctive features)
- **Match clothing and accessories** - character recognition through wardrobe consistency
- **Maintain age-appropriate appearance** - don't age up/down characters between scenes
- **Reference character profiles** - use `vice_studio/characters/[channel].json` for detailed appearance guidelines
- **Body language consistency** - character mannerisms, posture, movement style remains consistent

---

## Environment Authenticity

- **Authentic location details** - accurate architecture for setting (Miami Art Deco, modern glass towers, street-level grit)
- **Period and setting appropriate** - anachronisms break immersion (no 1920s cars in 2020s Miami)
- **Consistent architecture and design** - cohesive urban design language (either tropical, industrial, modern, etc.)
- **Realistic urban/natural elements** - vegetation, weather effects, street furniture, signage
- **No impossible or physics-breaking scenes** - gravity, water flow, structural integrity must be plausible
- **Logical scene geography** - spatial relationships make sense (can't see sun behind where it should be obscured)

---

## Color & Grade

- **Orange-teal default grading** - warm highlights (orange), cool shadows (teal) for cinematic look
- **Jewel tones for richness** - saturated but not oversaturated colors
- **High saturation in key elements** - draw eye to important objects through color emphasis
- **Desaturated backgrounds** - pull focus to subject through selective color
- **Consistent color science** - similar scenes maintain color consistency (morning light ≠ night light differently handled)
- **Motivated color shifts** - any color change serves narrative (sunset changes to night, neon changes mood)

---

## Technical Quality Standards

- **4K resolution minimum** - 3840 x 2160 pixels for source imagery
- **Proper aspect ratio** - 9:16 (1080 x 1920 px minimum for mobile)
- **No compression artifacts** - clean edges, no banding or color blocks
- **No AI artifacts** - if using AI generation, post-process to remove obvious AI tells (warped hands, impossible geometry)
- **Color accuracy** - proper white balance, accurate skin tones, believable material colors
- **Motion compatibility** - imagery works with intended motion/animation style

---
